Phase 2

Conducting On-the-Job Training (OJT)

Four-step OJT method — all tasks

▾
The Four-Step OJT Method
1
Prepare the trainee
Put the trainee at ease. State the task objective and its mission relevance. Ask what they already know. Connect new material to prior experience. Outline what they will be able to do at the end of the session.
2
Demonstrate and explain
Perform the task at normal pace, then at deliberate step-by-step pace while narrating. Reference the TTG, applicable regulation (DAFI 24-602V4, DTR chapter via DTR Index), DoW PPA guidance, and local SOP. Highlight common errors. Use actual equipment, systems, and forms wherever possible.
3
Supervised practice
Have the trainee perform the task while you observe. Do not intervene unless safety, data integrity, or customer harm is at risk. Ask the trainee to verbalize reasoning at key decision points. Allow recoverable mistakes — it accelerates learning. Provide corrective feedback after each attempt, not during.
4
Evaluate and document
When the trainee performs accurately, at acceptable speed, without prompting, evaluate against the go/no-go standard. Document completion in the training record system. For core tasks, obtain third-party certifier signature before closing the task.
Go/no-go standard: The trainee can perform the task independently, within local timeliness requirements, with correct use of procedures and documentation — without supervision or trainer reference.
Feedback & Coaching Best Practices
Provide structured, specific feedback after each training session
Use a consistent format: (1) what was done correctly, (2) what needs improvement and why, (3) specific corrective action for next session. Written feedback is preferred for complex tasks.
OPM Performance
Conduct initial work center orientation and review of trainee's training records
On the trainee's first day in the section, review the training record folder together. Confirm task status. Clarify the certification process and expectations.
Utilize training systems to monitor individual training progress
Maintain an up-to-date view of each trainee's task completion status. Flag trainees not progressing at expected pace. Conduct monthly progress reviews and document results.
