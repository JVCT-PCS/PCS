1. Authority & purpose

This guide provides standardized procedures for all Joint Personal Property Shipping Office (JPPSO), Personal Property Shipping Office (PPSO), and Personal Property Processing Office (PPPO) personnel when entering Transportation Account Codes (TACs), Lines of Accounting (LOAs), and Order Numbers into the Defense Personal Property System (DPS) and the Electronic Transportation Personal Property Standard System (eTOPS).

Properly entered LOA data ensures uninterrupted electronic bill payment through the DoW Transportation Payment and Processing System (TPPS/SYNCADA). These processes reimburse Transportation Service Providers (TSPs) for services rendered, provide financial visibility to stakeholders, and reduce paper documentation.

4. Critical LOA data entry rules

These rules apply to every service branch and every system entry. Failure to follow them results in payment delays, wrong-year billing, and failed electronic transactions.

#RuleDetail
1No letter "O" in TACsTACs never contain the letter O. What appears to be an O is always the digit 0. Example: *G67J entered incorrectly — correct entry is G67J.
2No special charactersDo not enter * # () AA 00 O or blank spaces in the fiscal year position or anywhere in the LOA string when copying and pasting.
3No leading zeros on DI097... is wrong — enter 97.... Similarly 096 NA... → enter 96 NA....
4Never create an SDNIf the SDN is missing from orders, return orders to the member. The counselor must NOT fabricate or construct an SDN. Characters after "CIC:" are not an SDN.
5No extra spaces when pastingVisually inspect every pasted TAC or LOA string. A single trailing space will cause entry failure or wrong payment routing.
6TAC always uppercaseAll TAC entries must be uppercase. Army TACs always begin with C and end with a number (e.g., CAG5).
7MDCs are not TACsArmy MDCs (e.g., 3AE5) must be converted to TACs (e.g., CAE5) using the crosswalk before entry. MDCs are never entered into DPS or eTOPS directly.
8NTS TAC fiscal year digitNTS TACs for Navy and Marine Corps include a fiscal year digit. For FY26 replace underscores with 6 (e.g., Navy NT_ → NT6). Services rendered in different fiscal years must use the TAC for the year services were rendered.
9Order Number exactly as writtenFor Air Force entries, the Order Number in the SAC/Ship. Acct. Class field must be entered exactly as it appears on the member's orders — character for character.
10Use base order TAC & SDN (Army)For Army IPPS-A orders, always use the TAC and SDN from the base order. Never use TAC or SDN from an amendment.

5. Army — DI 21

⭐
Overview

Army shipments use Department Indicator 21. All DPS and eTOPS entries require a TAC. MDCs from orders must be converted using the crosswalk. The 14-digit SDN is mandatory on all active duty Army orders and must be entered in the SAC field.

Never enter an MDC directly
When DPS prompts for an MDC, enter the converted TAC instead. Never enter the raw MDC code.
⭐
Active duty — IPPS-A PCS orders (DPS entry)

CONUS

  1. Select Department Indicator: 21 – United States Army
  2. Select Fund Type: MDC
  3. In "Enter MDC" field type the HHG TAC (converted from MDC crosswalk, e.g., CAE5) and click Validate
  4. If validation error appears, click Override TGET-R Information
  5. Enter MDC code in both MDC fields; enter HHG TAC in both TAC fields
  6. Enter the 14-digit SDN in the Shipment Accounting Classification field
  7. Click OK

OCONUS

  1. Select DI: 21 – United States Army
  2. Select Fund Type: TAC
  3. Enter TAC (e.g., CAG6) and click Validate
  4. Click Override TGET-R Information
  5. Enter MDC in both MDC fields; enter TAC in both TAC fields; enter SDN in SAC field
  6. Click OK
Example — active duty CONUS (IPPS-A order)
Order LOA: MDC 3AE5  |  HHG TAC (crosswalk): CAE5  |  SDN: HER5469PF82747
DPS entry → MDC fields: 3AE5  |  TAC fields: CAE5  |  SAC: HER5469PF82747

eTOPS NTS entry

  1. Search customer in MEMBER screen
  2. Arrow down to the current order number
  3. TAB through to the TAC field; type the correct NTS TAC; TAB to Commit and select BASIC
  4. In BASIC screen: enter the SDN in the SAC field and complete the record

Army civilian orders

Civilian orders may use a long LOA without a standalone TAC. OCONUS civilian moves require both a TAC and the SAC field populated.

  1. Identify DI from first two characters of the LOA
  2. In DPS select Fund Type: MDC; enter TAC in SDN field and validate
  3. If no TAC on orders, leave MDC field blank; enter the full long LOA in the SAC field
  4. If TAC is present: enter TAC in both MDC and TAC fields; enter full long LOA in SAC

Not a TAC

6. Navy — DI 17

⚓
Navy active duty

DPS allows entry of the TAC and/or full LOA — always select Fund Type TAC. For eTOPS use the TAC and SAC. The SAC line must include the SDN. NTS TACs include a fiscal year digit — always update for the correct FY.

DPS entry

  1. Select DI: 17 – United States Navy / United States Marine Corps
  2. Select Fund Type: TAC
  3. Enter HHG TAC (e.g., NAU6) and click Validate
  4. If TAC validates and populates fields — proceed to SAC; do NOT override
  5. If validation error appears: click Override TGET-R Information; enter TAC in TAC field; enter full LOA + SDN in SAC field

eTOPS NTS entry

  1. Enter NTS TAC in the Transportation Account Code field
  2. In BASIC screen: enter the full SAC LOA followed by the NTS SDN in the Ship. Acct. Class field
  3. Replace underscores in NTS SDN with current FY last digit (FY26 → 6)
NTS fiscal year digit
Navy NTS TAC and SDN include a FY digit. Example FY26: TAC NT6, SDN N00022 CBSNT6. Confirm and update at the start of each fiscal year.
Navy active duty LOA example
LOA: 1761453.2258 210 00022 050120 2D U74200 02226CTWDM06
SDN: N0002226CTWDM06  |  HHG TAC: NAU6
NTS TAC: NT6  |  NTS SDN: N00022 CBSNT6
SAC (eTOPS): 1761453.2258 210 00022 050120 2D U74200 02226CTWDM06 N00022 CBSNT6
⚓
Navy civilian orders

DPS allows TAC or full LOA entry. Select TAC unless there is no TAC on the orders; in that case enter the full LOA in the SAC field. For eTOPS use TAC and correct SAC for each shipment type.

Navy civilian example
LOA: AA 1791804 4A6A 210 66021 0 050120 2D 000000 A00004665424
HHG/UB TAC: NVPH  |  NTS TAC: NVFN  |  SAC: LOA above

7. Air Force / Space Force — DI 57

✈
AF / Space Force — PCS, retirement & separation (PPA HQ Advisory 26-002)

All AF and Space Force orders must contain a TAC. AF TACs typically begin with the letter G (e.g., G57E for HHG, GA7N for NTS).

PPA HQ Advisory 26-002 — Effective Immediately (10 March 2026)
In accordance with PPA HQ Advisory 26-002 dated 10 March 2026, all transportation personnel must follow the updated requirements below and reference the attached Data Entry Instructions when processing Air Force and Space Force PCS, Retirement, and Separation Orders:

In DPS:
  • Enter ONLY the HHG TAC in the seven specified fields.
  • Enter the Order Number exactly as it appears on the member's orders into the Shipment Accounting Classification (SAC) field.
In eTOPS:
  • Enter the applicable NTS or HHG TAC in the Transportation Account Code field.
  • Enter the Order Number exactly as it appears on the member's orders into the Ship. Acct. Class field.

DPS — 3-step process

  1. Step 1 — Validate: Select DI: 57 – United States Air Force / United States Space Force  |  Fund Type: SDN (only option)  |  Enter SDN field: input the HHG TAC code  |  Click Validate
  2. Step 2 — Override: A validation error will appear (no valid AIN found). Click Override TGET-R Information
  3. Step 3 — Populate: Enter HHG TAC in SDN field (x2)  |  Enter HHG TAC in AIN field (x2)  |  Enter HHG TAC in TAC field (x2)  |  Enter the Order Number exactly as it appears on orders in the Shipment Accounting Classification field  |  Click OK
Order Number — character perfect
The Order Number in the SAC field must be entered exactly as printed on the member's orders. No extra spaces, no alterations. When copying and pasting, verify no trailing space was captured.

eTOPS — NTS entry

  1. Search customer in MEMBER screen; arrow down to current order number
  2. Change HHG TAC to NTS TAC; TAB to commit; select BASIC
  3. In BASIC screen: enter NTS TAC in Transportation Account Code field; enter NTS TAC in Ship. Acct. Class field
AF active duty example
Order Number: AS-030895  |  HHG TAC: F7E1
DPS: SDN=F7E1, Re-Enter SDN=F7E1, AIN=F7E1, Re-Enter AIN=F7E1, TAC=F7E1, Re-Enter TAC=F7E1, SAC=AS-030895
eTOPS NTS: TAC=GA7N  |  Ship. Acct. Class=GA7N
✈
Air National Guard / Air Force Reserve

ANG and AFRES orders use DI 57. All AF orders must contain a TAC — if no TAC is present, contact PPA HQ Operations at ppahq.af.workflow@us.af.mil.

DPS entry (ANG/Reserve)

  1. Select DI: 57  |  Fund Type: SDN
  2. Enter SDN (the MORD/order number from Supporting LOA on orders) in SDN field; enter AIN; enter HHG TAC in TAC field
  3. Enter Shipment Accounting Classification (full LOA string from orders)

eTOPS entry (ANG/Reserve NTS)

  1. Search customer in MEMBER screen; change HHG TAC to NTS TAC
  2. Arrow down to current order number; TAB to TAC; type correct TAC; TAB to commit; select BASIC
  3. In BASIC screen: enter SDN in SAC field and complete record

🦅
Marine Corps active duty

Marine Corps orders use DI 17 (shared with Navy). The correct HHG/UB SDN always contains "CT" in the 9th and 10th character positions. NTS and POV Storage TACs are annual and must be updated each fiscal year.

DPS entry

  2. Select Fund Type: TAC
  3. Enter HHG TAC and click Validate

  5. Click OK
Marine Corps DPS entry

  2. Arrow down to current order number; TAB to TAC; enter NTS TAC; TAB to commit; select BASIC
  3. In BASIC screen: enter NTS SDN in Ship. Acct. Class field; complete record
NTS expiration date
NTS expiration date is always the end of the current fiscal year (30 Sep) regardless of when the member placed items into storage or the length of their tour.

11. AROWS — Air National Guard PCS entitlements on active service orders

This section covers the Air National Guard Reserve Order Writing System (AROWS) procedures for authorizing Permanent Change of Station (PCS) entitlements on Active Service Orders per AROWS Job Aid 0418. Applies to: AGR Orders Specialist, School Orders Specialist, Stat Tour Human Resource Manager, and Unit Order Specialist.

✦
Step 1 — Creating a new PCS order application
  1. Log in to AROWS and use Change Profile to ensure your profile is set to the correct Orders Specialist role (AGR, School, or Unit OS)
  2. Click Please Select A Menu → Individual Orders → New Application
  3. On the New Application screen: enter the SSN; look up and select the appropriate order sub-type; select Yes to the question "Is this a Permanent Change of Station (PCS) order?"
  4. Click Create Application
Cannot change after creation
The response to the PCS question cannot be changed after clicking Create Application — except for AGR Continuation orders. To change: the order must be deleted (published orders must be cancelled) and re-created. Selecting Yes allows entitlements to be added on future modifications even if none are added initially.
Automatic PCS authorization
AGR Initial Tour, AGR Continuation Tour, and Formal Schools Travel PCS Only order types automatically authorize PCS. Entitlements on these orders are optional and can be added or changed on modifications.
PCS paragraph auto-generated
Selecting Yes prints the following on the order automatically: "This is a PCS approved order authorized by (JTR 050203). Dependents: ___. Number of Cars authorized: ___. Member is authorized ___ travel day(s)."
✦
Step 2 — Personnel Info tab (dependents & vehicles)
  1. Enter the number of cars authorized for reimbursement in the "Number of cars authorized" field
  2. Check the box next to each dependent who will travel with the member
  3. Review all dependent information for accuracy — if any field states "not set," contact your AROWS Profile Editor to update
  4. After information is updated, click Refresh Personnel Info to update the order application
✦
Step 3 — Duty Locations tab (travel days, per diem & transportation)
  1. Enter the Travel Start Date and Initial Report Date — the number of travel days printed is determined by these two dates. The first day of a PCS order is a travel day. Example: Travel Start = 10/25, Initial Report = 10/26 → 2 travel days.
  2. Click the Select hyperlink next to "Travel & Per Diem Commuting Status Information"
  3. Always select: "PCS, AGR/Pipeline Student/Stat Tour, will commute, no per diem authorized" for all PCS orders
  4. Select the mode of transportation:
    • OCONUS PCS → Commercial Air
    • CONUS PCS → Personal Vehicle – Adv to Govt

Step 6 — Funding screen (LOA, TAC & MORD entry)

The Funding screen contains Pay & Allowance citations and the Supporting LOA table for PCS/HHG funding. If a PCS & HHG box appears, return to the HHG screen and remove HHG entitlements first.

Travel & Per Diem fund citation

  1. Selecting PCS per diem status on the Duty Locations screen automatically generates the Travel & Per Diem fund citation fields
  2. Only one Travel & Per Diem funding line (corresponding to order start date) is allowed
  3. Contact your Financial Management Office for the correct citation — the Budget Analyst must create specific lines of accounting for Travel and Per Diem

Supporting LOA (HHG / PCS funding)

  1. Select a Supporting LOA Type — your Finance office may need to coordinate with NGB/FM for the correct type
  2. Enter Supporting LOA Cost — no comma or dollar sign ($)
  3. In the Supporting LOA Funding Source field: enter the LOA followed by the TAC and MORD Number (type in or copy/paste)
  4. Click ADD to load the LOA
  5. Multiple Supporting LOA lines may be added for additional lines or for a single LOA split across two lines
Coordinate with Finance
Always coordinate with your Finance office for the correct Supporting LOA Type, LOA, TAC, and MORD number before completing the Funding screen.

13. Correcting an LOA in DPS

Use this 6-step process when an LOA has already been submitted and requires correction.

  1. In DPS, navigate to Shipment Management in the left navigation menu
  2. Under Search/Reports, click Find Shipments/Customers
  3. Enter the customer's DoD ID or last 4 SSN plus last name; click Search
  4. Locate the correct record and click View/Edit Shipment
  5. Scroll down to the Line of Accounting Information section and click Edit
  6. Enter the corrected TAC; click Override TGET-R Information if needed; update all corrected fields; update the Shipment Accounting Classification; click Submit
DI cannot be corrected
The Department Indicator cannot be changed after counselor submission regardless of circumstances. If the wrong DI was selected, the shipment must be fully cancelled and re-counseled from scratch.
eTOPS LOA correction
To correct an LOA in eTOPS: go to Shipment Management → Search/Reports → Find Shipments/Customers → View/Edit Shipment → scroll to Line of Accounting Information → Edit → enter TAC → Override TGET-R Information → enter corrected fields → enter Shipment Accounting Classification → Submit.
