1. Fundamentals & key terms

Every shipment processed through DPS or eTOPS requires accurate accounting information. Errors delay payment to Transportation Service Providers (TSPs) and can result in incorrect fiscal year charges. The following fields appear across all service branches:

AbbreviationFull nameWhat it is
DIDepartment IndicatorFirst 2 characters of the LOA. Identifies the paying agency — NOT necessarily the member's branch of service.
TACTransportation Account Code4-character code that funds the shipment. Always uppercase, never contains the letter O, Army TACs always start with C and end with a number (e.g., CAE5).
SDNStandard Document Number14-character reference number linking the shipment to the obligation. Character-perfect — never create or guess one.
MDCMovement Designator CodeArmy-specific code on orders. MDCs are NOT TACs and are NOT entered into DPS or eTOPS directly — convert using the crosswalk table.
AINAuthorized Identification NumberDFAS Station Code — used for Air Force and some Navy entries.
SACShipment Account ClassificationThe "long line of accounting." In DPS this is the Shipment Accounting Classification field; in eTOPS it is the Ship. Acct. Class field.
LOALine of AccountingThe full accounting string. DI is always the first 2 characters.
NTSNon-Temporary StorageStorage funded under PCS entitlements. Uses a separate TAC from HHG shipments.
HHGHousehold GoodsPersonal property shipment entitlement.
POVPrivately Owned VehicleVehicle storage/shipment. Requires its own TAC.
Golden rule
The Department Indicator identifies who is paying, not who is moving. Always read the LOA on the orders before selecting DI — a soldier moving OCONUS may be funded by Army Corps of Engineers (DI 96), not Army (DI 21).

3. Army — DI 21

⭐
Active Duty (IPPS-A orders)
Integrated Personnel Pay System – Army

For IPPS-A PCS orders, the SDN is always on the base order and is always correct. The TAC also appears on the base order if shipment or storage is authorized.

Critical
Always use the TAC and SDN from the base order, never from an amendment. The base order data was used to create the obligation.

DPS entry — active duty (CONUS)

  1. Select Department Indicator: 21 – United States Army
  2. Select Fund Type: MDC (even though you will enter a TAC)
  3. In the "Enter MDC" field, type the HHG TAC (e.g., CAE5) and click Validate
  4. If validation fails, click Override TGET-R Information
  5. Enter the MDC code in the MDC field (both boxes), and the TAC in the TAC field (both boxes)
  6. Enter the 14-digit SDN in the Shipment Accounting Classification field
  7. Click OK
Example — active duty CONUS order
MDC on order: 3AE5  |  HHG TAC (crosswalk): CAE5  |  SDN: HER5469PF82747
DPS entry → MDC field: 3AE5  |  TAC field: CAE5  |  SAC: HER5469PF82747

eTOPS (NTS) entry

  1. Search customer in MEMBER screen
  2. Arrow down to current order number
  3. TAB to TAC field — type correct TAC, TAB to Commit, select BASIC
  4. In BASIC screen: enter SDN in the SAC field and complete the record

MDC ≠ TAC
MDCs are never entered into DPS or eTOPS. Convert MDC → TAC using this crosswalk before data entry. TACs always start with C and end with a number. TACs never contain the letter O.

⭐
Army civilian orders

Army civilian orders may not always contain an explicit TAC. Civilians moving OCONUS require the SAC field to be populated in addition to the TAC.

  1. Identify DI from the first two characters of the LOA on the orders
  2. In DPS, select Fund Type: MDC
  3. Enter the TAC in the SDN field and validate; enter the TAC again in both MDC and TAC fields
  4. Enter the full long LOA in the SAC field

4. Navy — DI 17

⚓
Navy active duty & civilians

DPS allows entry of the TAC and/or full LOA — always select TAC as fund type. For eTOPS, use the TAC and enter the SAC with the SDN included.

NTS — fiscal year digit required
Navy NTS TACs include a fiscal year digit. For FY26, the NTS TAC would read NT6 (replacing the underscore with the last digit of the current fiscal year). Always add the correct FY digit.

DPS entry

  1. Select Department Indicator: 17 – United States Navy / United States Marine Corps
  2. Select Fund Type: TAC
  3. Enter the HHG TAC (e.g., NAU6) and click Validate
  4. If TAC validates and populates fields — proceed. Do NOT override unless a validation error appears
  5. If validation error appears: click Override TGET-R Information, enter TAC in TAC field, enter full LOA in SAC field

eTOPS (NTS) entry

  1. Enter NTS TAC in the Transportation Account Code field
  2. Enter the full SAC LOA followed by the NTS SDN in the Ship. Acct. Class field
  3. Add the current FY digit to the NTS SDN where underscores appear (e.g., N00022__CBSNT6 → N00022 CBSNT6)
Navy active duty LOA example
LOA: 1761453.2258 210 00022 050120 2D U74200 02226CTWDM06
SDN: N0002226CTWDM06  |  HHG TAC: NAU6
NTS TAC: NT6  |  NTS SDN: N00022 CBSNT6  |  SAC LOA: full LOA above

5. Air Force / Space Force — DI 57

✈
Air Force PCS, retirement & separation orders

All Air Force orders must contain a TAC. Effective immediately per PPA HQ Advisory 26-002, the following procedures apply to all AF and Space Force PCS, Retirement, and Separation orders.

DPS entry — 3 steps

  1. Step 1: Select DI: 57 – United States Air Force / United States Space Force  |  Fund Type: SDN (only option)  |  Enter SDN field: input the HHG TAC code  |  Click Validate
  2. Step 2: A validation error will appear (no AIN found). Click Override TGET-R Information
  3. Step 3: Enter HHG TAC code in SDN field (x2), AIN field (x2), and TAC field (x2). Enter the Order Number in the Shipment Accounting Classification field. Click OK
No extra spaces
When copying and pasting TACs, ensure no extra spaces are captured. The Order Number must be entered exactly as it appears on the member's orders.
AF active duty example
HHG TAC: G57J  |  NTS TAC: GA7N  |  Order Number: AS-030895
DPS: SDN=G57J, AIN=G57J, TAC=G57J, SAC=AS-030895
eTOPS: TAC=GA7N (NTS), Ship. Acct. Class=GA7N

eTOPS entry (NTS)

  1. Enter the NTS TAC (or HHG TAC for local moves/OTO DPM) in the Transportation Account Code field
  2. Enter the same TAC in the Ship. Acct. Class field

Air National Guard / Air Force Reserve

ANG/AFRES orders use DI 57 and follow the same DPS process. In DPS, enter the SDN, AIN, and TAC; in eTOPS, enter the NTS TAC and SDN.

ANG / Reserve example
HHG LOA: 5753510GP5480Z58480N05525725  |  SDN: PB584150GPMP0H
HHG TAC: G54H  |  NTS TAC: GH4N  |  AIN: 525725

6. Marine Corps — DI 17

🦅
Marine Corps active duty

Marine Corps orders use DI 17 (same as Navy). The correct SDN always contains "CT" in the 9th and 10th positions.

Where to find the TAC and SDN on orders

FieldLocation on ordersExample
HHG TACListed in the LOA table under "Household Goods"M7B5
NTS TACListed under "Non-Temp Storage" in the LOA tableM935
SDN (for HHG/UB)Look for "CT" in positions 9–10 of the SDN stringM7000225CTBG9XA
SDN selection tip
Marines may have multiple SDNs on their orders. The correct one for HHG/UB always has CT in the 9th and 10th positions.

DPS entry

  1. Select Department Indicator: 17 – United States Navy / United States Marine Corps
  2. Select Fund Type: TAC
  3. Enter the HHG TAC and validate
  4. Enter the CT-SDN in the Shipment Accounting Classification field
Marine Corps DPS entry
DI: 17  |  TAC: M7B5  |  SAC: M7000225CTBG9XA

NTS TAC — FY digit required

Marine Corps NTS and POV Storage TACs are annual — the last digit reflects the fiscal year. For FY26, see the TAC table in Section 9.

Annual TAC update
NTS and POV storage TACs must be updated in LASER and TPPS/SYNCADA at the start of each new fiscal year. Services rendered Oct 25 – Sep 26 use FY26 TACs.

8. OSD / DLA / Army Corps of Engineers / Other — DI 96 & 97

🏛
Army Corps of Engineers — DI 96

LOA begins with 96. Always select DI 96 for Army Corps shipments even if the LOA also references Army (DI 21) TAC codes.

  1. Select DI: 96 – Army Corps of Engineers
  2. Select Fund Type: TAC
  3. Enter HHG TAC and validate
  4. Enter full LOA in SAC field. Do not accept orders with no TAC
Zero in LOA
Army Corps long LOAs sometimes show leading zeros. Do NOT enter those zeros: 096 NA X X... should be entered as 96 NA X X...
Army Corps example
LOA: 096 NA X X 4902 000 0000 08 2450 NA S96041 2LB841
Enter as: 96 NA X X 4902 000 0000 08 2450 NA S96041 2LB841  |  TAC: C34A
🏛
OSD / DLA / Defense Agencies — DI 97

LOA begins with 97. These include DoD OSD, DLA, and other defense agency shipments. Normally has a 10-digit SDN.

  1. Select DI: 97 – Office of the Secretary of Defense
  2. Select Fund Type: TAC
  3. Enter HHG TAC and validate; if error, override and enter LOA in SAC
Multiple zeros
OSD orders sometimes contain long LOAs with many consecutive zeros. Remove all extra zeros — only use one 0 in the long LOA where appropriate.
OSD / DLA example
LOA: 97 2025 0100 4801 FSN78803 121D FPPC21GXXXXXXXX 61000100
Wrong entry: 097 2025 0100... → Correct: 97 2025 0100...
TAC: HMAG  |  SDN: PC5022 4500

10. Correcting an LOA in DPS

Use this process when an LOA has already been submitted and needs correction.

  1. In DPS, navigate to Shipment Management in the left menu
  2. Under Search/Reports, click Find Shipments/Customers
  3. Enter the customer's DoD ID or last 4 SSN plus last name, then click Search
  4. Click View/Edit Shipment next to the relevant record
  5. Scroll down to the Line of Accounting Information section and click Edit
  6. Enter the corrected TAC, click Override TGET-R Information, enter corrected fields, update the Shipment Accounting Classification, and click Submit
Tip
The Department Indicator cannot be changed after counselor submission. If the wrong DI was selected, the shipment must be cancelled and re-counseled.
